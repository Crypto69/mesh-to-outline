What we collect: nothing

Mesh to Outline runs only on your computer, inside Autodesk Fusion. It does not send your designs, meshes, settings or any other information over the internet. It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising, and it works without an internet connection.

Third parties

None. Autodesk handles your download through the Autodesk Marketplace under Autodesk's own privacy statement.

Storage and deletion

The add-in stores nothing outside your Fusion design: the sketches and bodies it makes are part of your design, like anything you draw yourself. Uninstalling the add-in removes it completely.
